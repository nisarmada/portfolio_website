I'm passionate about building efficient systems and tackling complex problems — a mindset forged through a BSc in Nuclear Physics and sharpened by two years of low-level programming at Codam.

My internship at Leaning Technologies is the work I'm most proud of: designing a TypeScript test suite that exposed silent C++ failures, then going deep into the codebase to fix the bugs it uncovered. That mix of automation and low-level debugging is where I do my best work.

I'm proactive about picking up new stacks quickly when a project needs them.
